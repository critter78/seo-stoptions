## Voice & tone

- Confident, expert, no-nonsense.
- Avoid hype words: "revolutionary", "groundbreaking", "game-changer", "unlock", "supercharge".
- Use specific numbers wherever possible — never "a lot" or "many".
- Write for intermediate-to-advanced traders, not beginners.

- **Content depth** — long-form, original-data-driven posts; thin pages get rewritten or removed.
